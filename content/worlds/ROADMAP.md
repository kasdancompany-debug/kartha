# World roadmap

Launch worlds (Phase 1 StyleKits): The Ancient Banyan Forest (Gond), The Himalayan Kingdom (Pahari/Kangra miniature), The Lost Stepwell (Rajasthani/Gujarati block print), The Monsoon City (contemporary printmaking + Bombay sign-painting), The Star Observatory (Deccani miniature), The Secret Jungle (Mithila).

Future worlds, each needing its own cultural consultant and commissioned artist before it ships:

- **Pattachitra** (Odisha coast) — scroll-painting tradition, narrative borders.
- **Warli** (Sahyadri hills, Maharashtra) — geometric figures, harvest and village-life scenes, not ritual.
- **Kalamkari** (Andhra Pradesh) — pen-and-natural-dye narrative cloth painting.
- **Bhil** (Madhya Pradesh / Rajasthan / Gujarat) — dot-pointillist painting.
- **Sohrai** (Jharkhand) — finger-painted mural tradition using natural earth pigments.
- **Tholu Bommalata** (Andhra Pradesh) — leather shadow-puppetry, translated to a silhouette/light StyleKit.
- **Kerala backwaters** — a coastal/water-trade world; tradition TBD with consultant.

Each new world follows the same rule as the launch six: **one world = one visual tradition**, no mixing, no religious figures or symbols, consultant sign-off recorded in `/content/CULTURAL_REVIEW.md` before any art is commissioned.
