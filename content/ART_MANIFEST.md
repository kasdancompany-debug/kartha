Companion rigs (`components/companions/*`) and world scenes (`components/worlds/scenes/*`) landed in Phases 2-3 (rows above); Phase 4 closed the two gaps that work left open:

1. **Parallax wiring.** `WorldWindow` (`components/worlds/WorldWindow.tsx`) now takes a `layers: Record<string, ReactNode>` prop keyed by each world's `LayerSpec.id` instead of an opaque `children` node, looks up each layer's `depth` from `kit.parallaxLayers`, and renders them through the same `ParallaxLayers`/`useParallaxOffset` mechanism the Landing backdrop (`Backdrop.tsx`) already used — so hovering or tilting a world window now shifts its 5 authored layers at their own depths. The ambient layer was also fixed to render every entry in `kit.ambient[]` (e.g. Himalaya's mist *and* snow), not just the first.
2. **Companion × world styling.** `CompanionRigProps` gained an optional `worldId`; each of the 5 rig components now recolors its placeholder shapes from that world's `WorldStyleKit.palette` when set, and `WeaveLeftPage` (`components/oracle/steps/Weave.tsx`) renders the chosen companion in its chosen world's colours as the "all four threads are woven" payoff. This is a code-level styling mechanism, not the bespoke per-companion × per-world illustration set the manifest originally described — that remains real-artist work once a tradition is commissioned per world.
